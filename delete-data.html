Bash Grid

Delete your Bash Grid data

For the Android app "Bash Grid" (including Gem Cascade, Bloom Quest and Solitaire), made by an independent developer.

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Bash Grid.
  2. On the title screen, tap ✏️ Nickname.
  3. Tap Delete my leaderboard data, then tap it again to confirm.

You need an internet connection. Your data is deleted from our servers straight away.

Option 2: by email

If you no longer have the app, email bashgrid.games@gmail.com with the subject "Bash Grid data deletion" and your nickname with its 4-digit tag (for example BlockFan #1234), as shown on the leaderboard. We delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your nickname and 4-digit tag
  • Your scores on all world leaderboards
  • Your anonymous player ID (login)
  • By email request also: feedback messages you sent, and reports you made

What is kept

  • Nothing on our servers after deletion.
  • Your game progress, best scores and settings saved on your phone stay there until you uninstall the app or clear its storage (Settings > Apps > Bash Grid > Storage > Clear data).
  • Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

See also the full privacy policy.
