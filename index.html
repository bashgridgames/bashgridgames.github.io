Bash Grid

A free block puzzle game for Android.

Fit the blocks, clear the lines

Drag pieces onto the 8×8 grid and fill whole rows or columns to clear them. Every set of three pieces always fits, so think about the order! Three modes (Easy, Intermediate and Hard), each with its own world leaderboard.

Bonus games included

  • Gem Cascade: match-3, with Classic, Rush and Zen modes
  • Bloom Quest: 60 garden levels with goals and stars
  • Solitaire: Klondike, Spider and FreeCell
