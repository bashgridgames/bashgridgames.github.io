Bash Grid

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Bash Grid app ("the app", including its bonus games Gem Cascade, Bloom Quest and Solitaire) collects, why, and what you can do about it.

1. Who we are

Bash Grid is developed by an independent developer. Contact: bashgrid.games@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

a) World leaderboard

To show the world leaderboard, the app uses Google Firebase:

  • An anonymous player ID, created the first time the app needs to talk to the leaderboard (for example when you set a nickname, send feedback or report a name). It is a random code and is not linked to your name, email address, phone number or Google account.
  • The nickname you choose (shown publicly on the leaderboard).
  • A 4-digit tag made from your player ID (shown next to your nickname).
  • Your best score for each game mode that has a leaderboard (shown publicly on the leaderboard).

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

b) Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and general location (country or region), and how you interact with ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice later by clearing the app's storage (Settings > Apps > Bash Grid > Storage > Clear data) and opening the app again. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Google's use of this data is described here: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

c) Feedback

If you send a message with the Feedback button, we receive: the message you type, the type you picked (problem, idea or other), your nickname, the app version, your anonymous player ID and your phone's browser/model description. It is stored in Google Firebase and only the developer can read it. Please don't put personal details in the message. We use feedback only to fix problems and improve the app, and delete it when it is no longer needed (at most 2 years).

d) Reports of offensive names

If you tap another player's name on the leaderboard and choose Report, we receive your anonymous player ID, the reported player's ID, their nickname and the leaderboard. Only the developer can read reports. We use them only to remove offensive nicknames, and delete them once checked.

e) Information stored on your phone

Your best scores, game progress, settings, nickname, a copy of the last loaded leaderboards and any unsent feedback draft are also saved on your phone so the game remembers them. This data stays on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. How we use the information

  • To run the world leaderboard and show your rank.
  • To read and answer feedback, and fix the problems reported.
  • To prevent cheating and remove offensive nicknames or false scores.
  • To show ads, which keep the game free.

We do not sell your information.

4. Where the information is stored

Leaderboard data is stored by Google Firebase on servers in the European Union. Google acts as our data processor. Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. How long we keep it

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard exists, or until you delete them. Entries that break the rules (for example offensive nicknames or cheating) may be changed or deleted at any time.

6. Your rights and deleting your data

You can delete your data yourself in the app: tap Nickname on the title screen, then Delete my leaderboard data. This deletes your nickname, your scores on the world leaderboards and your anonymous player ID from our servers straight away. Your progress saved on your phone stays until you uninstall the app. More details: Delete your data.

You can also ask us to delete your data (including any feedback you sent) or change your nickname by emailing bashgrid.games@gmail.com with your nickname and 4-digit tag. We do this within 30 days. If you live in the EU/EEA or UK, you also have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: bashgrid.games@gmail.com
